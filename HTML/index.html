What is Study Planner ?

Study Planner is a simple productivity tool that helps students organize subjects, manage tasks, and keep track of deadlines in one place. Stay focused, reduce stress, and spend more time learning instead of worrying about what comes next.

Dashboard

Simple - Effective

Get a clear overview of your subjects and remaining tasks in one place. Stay informed about your workload, priorities, and upcoming deadlines without unnecessary complexity.

Subject Management

Colorful - Easy to use

Create, organize, and manage your subjects with custom colors. Keep your study space structured and visually clear while seeing how many tasks remain for each subject.

Task Management

Modern - Productive

Create, prioritize, and manage tasks with deadlines, specific times, and subjects. Mark completed tasks instantly and keep your workload organized by priority for a more productive study routine.
